Train your own real-time sleep spindle detector

A hands-on course for neuroscientists. You will learn how the Portiloop turns raw EEG into a closed-loop stimulation decision in a fraction of a second, how its neural network is trained on expert-annotated sleep recordings, and how to train, evaluate and deploy a model of your own. Every concept comes with a simulation you can play with directly in this page.

The published Portiloop network, running in your browser

Synthetic NREM sleep EEG goes through the Portiloop filters and the pre-trained network (25,679 parameters, the model of the PLOS ONE paper). Shaded bands are the spindles we planted in the signal; the orange trace is the network's spindle probability, computed on the fly from past samples only. Press New recording to draw another night.

How these tutorials work

  • Interactive widgets (boxes marked ▶) run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: even when you load your own file in tutorial 09, it stays on your computer.
  • The network in the widgets is the real one. Its weights were exported from the published PyTorch checkpoint and checked against PyTorch to within 10-6 (see docs/tools/ in the repository).
  • The EEG in the widgets is synthetic: a didactic signal with planted spindles, so that the ground truth is known exactly. It is not a physiological model, and numbers measured on it (F1 scores, delays) are illustrations, not results.
  • Code blocks are taken from the repository or tested against it. Commands assume you are at the root of a clone of Portiloop/portiloop-training.
  • Callouts marked On the hard drive tell you which files are read and written, and where.
